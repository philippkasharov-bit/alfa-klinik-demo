# Alfa-Klinik — 3 redesign directions (hero only)

Prototypes: `public/proposals/{a,b,c}.html` on branch `feat/redesign-directions`. Screenshots: `A|B|C-desktop.png` (1440), `A|B|C-mobile.png` (390). All three use only the generated 3D tooth renders and type; there are no photos of people, staff or interiors. Copy is the clinic's own.

## A — «Атлас» (clinical atlas)
- **Thesis:** the site reads like a precise anatomical atlas. Each service is a "figure" with numbered callouts, so it feels trustworthy, calm and expert.
- **Type:** Literata (serif display) + IBM Plex Sans (body) + IBM Plex Mono (figure numbers and measurements only). All three support Cyrillic.
- **Palette:** paper #F2EEE6 · ink #1B1A17 · muted #5E5A52 · rule #CFC7B8 · oxide #A8432C.
- **Motion:** the tooth settles into the plate with a short rotation. Callout lines draw in one after another, and as you scroll, the 3D model turns to the view each section needs.

## B — «Фарфор» (porcelain)
- **Thesis:** bright, clinical, confident modernism. Huge tight sans headline, the tooth floating through the type, one cobalt accent for the action.
- **Type:** Onest (300–600; Cyrillic grotesk), a single family.
- **Palette:** white #FFFFFF · ink #0E0F10 · porcelain #ECEFF1 · mid #6B7277 · cobalt #2747D8.
- **Motion:** the tooth floats slowly in front of the headline and tilts with the cursor or scroll (3D model). The headline lines slide in staggered.

## C — «Тишина» (night quiet)
- **Thesis:** the premium private-practice mood: a dark, hushed room with the tooth lit like a jewellery piece. Slow, reassuring, high-end.
- **Type:** Prata (display serif) + Golos Text (body). Both support Cyrillic.
- **Palette:** night #0F1513 · deep #18211E · bone #EDE7DA · dim #A39E92 · brass #B89B6A.
- **Motion:** a soft spotlight follows the cursor (live in the prototype). Hovering a button fills it with brass. The 3D tooth turns slowly in the light.

## Recommendation
**A — «Атлас».** It turns the missing clinic photography into the concept itself (figures, callouts, measurements), so the absence of photos reads as intentional. It is the most ownable of the three and scales to services, prices and doctors without stock imagery. B is the safest commercially but closest to generic tech landing pages. C has the strongest mood, but a dark theme can work against a dental clinic's "clean" expectation.
